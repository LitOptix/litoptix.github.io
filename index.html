What records support causation for the surgery?
Three records form the causation chain: (1) the pre-incident MRI showing only mild degeneration with no herniation (Bayview Imaging, 11/19/2023, BIC00007); (2) the same-day ER presentation with new radicular symptoms (St. Mercy ED, 03/15/2024, SME00112); and (3) the post-incident MRI documenting a new L4-5 herniation contacting the L5 nerve root (Bayview Imaging, 04/02/2024, BIC00019) — the level later operated on (Harborview Ortho, 07/28/2024, HOS00233). Note one defense issue: a 52-day treatment gap before the surgical consult.
